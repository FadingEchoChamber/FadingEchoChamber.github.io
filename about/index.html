为什么建站？
创建这个网站，是希望有一个能积累知识与兴趣的地方。与他人分享，会让这些积累慢慢沉淀。如果还能帮助到更多人、解决大家的问题，那就更好了。
与大多数垂直类的技术博客不同，这里的内容更加多元：有技能的教程干货，有生活里的吐槽与妙招，也有对话题的思考与想法。凡是自己研究过、发现过的，都会分享在这里。
这就是建站的本意，也是我分享生活的方式。很幸运能与你在这里相遇，愿我们共同留下一段美好的回忆。

I created this site as a place to accumulate knowledge and interests. Sharing what I learn with others turns these into something that lasts. If it can also help more people and solve their problems, then it is even better.
Unlike most vertical tech blogs, the content here is more diverse: technical tutorials and know-how, small tips and thoughts from daily life, and reflections on all kinds of topics. Whatever I study or discover, I share it here.
This is the original purpose of this site, and it is also my way of sharing my life. I am glad to meet you here, and I hope we can create some good memories together.
